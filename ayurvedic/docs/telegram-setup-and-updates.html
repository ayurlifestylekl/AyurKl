Telegram Alerts — Setup Guide

KERALA AYURVEDIC LIFESTYLE  ·  STAFF NOTIFICATIONS & LATEST UPDATES

Prepared by Aurexis Solution

Your website can now send an instant Telegram message to your team whenever something needs attention — a new booking request, a payment received, or a cancellation. This guide shows you, step by step, how to switch it on by adding your bot token. The second half lists the other new features added in this update.

1. What you'll get

Once set up, your staff Telegram group receives a short message the moment any of these happen:

  • 🆕 New booking / consultation request — so the front desk can approve quickly.
  • ✅ Payment received — a booking is paid and confirmed.
  • ❌ Cancelled / auto-released — a booking was cancelled or its unpaid slot reopened.

These alerts are in addition to the emails customers already receive. No customer health details are ever sent to Telegram — only the name, therapy and time.

3. Step-by-step setup

  1. Create a Telegram group for your team
    Make a new Telegram group (e.g. “Kerala Bookings”) and add the staff who should get the alerts.
  2. Add the bot to the group
    In the group, tap Add members and search for @bookingkalsbot — add it like any other member.
  3. Wake the bot up
    In the group, send this exact message: /start@bookingkalsbot
    This lets the bot “see” the group so the website can find it in the next step.
  4. Open the website settings
    Log in to your website Admin, go to Settings, and scroll to the “Telegram staff alerts” section.
  5. Paste the bot token
    In the Bot token box, paste the token from BotFather (e.g. 8693911665:AAE3…).
  6. Detect your group
    Click Detect. The website reads your bot and lists the group(s) that messaged it. Click your group to select it — its Chat ID fills in automatically. (No copying long web links.)
  7. Save
    Click Save Telegram settings. You’ll see a green “Connected” label. That’s it — alerts start arriving straight away.

4. If “Detect” finds nothing

  • Make sure you sent /start@bookingkalsbot inside the group (not to yourself).
  • Confirm the bot is actually a member of the group.
  • Double-check the token was pasted fully, with no spaces before or after.
  • Send the /start@bookingkalsbot message once more, then click Detect again.

Tip: to test it end to end, make a booking request on the website — a “New request” message should appear in your group within a few seconds.

5. Also new in this update

Alongside Telegram alerts, this update added the following. Everything is already live.

Group bookings — a different therapy per guest NEW

A group booking no longer forces everyone into the same therapy. Each guest can choose their own therapy, is matched to their own same-gender therapist at the same time, and the group still pays once in a single bill. Your staff see each guest as Name · age · gender · therapy.

Smarter staff schedule
  • Block reasons show on the schedule — each blocked slot displays its reason at a glance.
  • Search your blocks — the Availability list has a search box to find a block by date, time, therapist or reason, so you can remove the right one quickly.
  • Block & unblock right on the schedule — tap a free 30-minute slot to block it (with a reason), or tap a block to remove it — no separate screen needed.
Customer announcements & closures NEW

From Front Desk → Announcements you can push a notice to the public website:

  • Closure (e.g. Deepavali) — shows a “we’re closed on [date]” banner to customers and automatically stops bookings on those dates. Remove it and bookings reopen.
  • Message — a free-text banner (holiday hours, a promotion) that doesn’t affect bookings.
Lead capture — grow your customer list NEW
  • A welcome pop-up greets first-time visitors and invites them to leave their name, email and phone for exclusive deals (they can dismiss it).
  • The WhatsApp button now asks for name, phone and email first — so every WhatsApp enquiry becomes a saved contact.
  • All captured details appear in Admin → Leads, with a Download (Excel) button.
More reliable payments

The booking payment flow was hardened so a paid booking always confirms — even if a payment notification is delayed or missed, the system re-checks with the payment gateway and confirms automatically, and the status page updates the moment payment lands.
