1. The approach — two tracks

Run both tracks together. Organic builds the brand and the content bank; Paid amplifies the best of it to a targeted local audience for bookings now.

ORGANIC Build trust & audience
  • Cost: time, not money
  • Goal: awareness, credibility, a content library
  • Channels: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp
  • Pace: 3–5 posts / week, steady
  • Wins compound over months
PAID Buy reach & bookings
  • Cost: ad budget (scalable)
  • Goal: fast, measurable bookings
  • Channels: Meta (IG/FB) + TikTok ads, retargeting
  • Pace: always-on + campaign bursts
  • Boost your best organic posts

Golden rule: never run ads to a cold audience with weak creative. Post organically first, see what gets saved / shared, then put budget behind the winners.

4. Organic track — content system

Post around five content pillars so the feed stays varied and purposeful. Aim for 3–5 posts a week, mostly short video.

PillarMixExamples
Educate30%"What's your dosha?", benefits of Panchakarma, Ayurveda for sleep/stress, myth-busting
Show the experience25%Shirodhara oil pour, herbal prep, the calm space, an abhyanga massage in motion
Proof & trust20%Client testimonials, before/after wellbeing, Google reviews on screen, the Vaidya's story
Offers & action15%First-visit offer, referral deal, festival packages, "slots this week"
Heritage & brand10%Authentic Kerala roots, sourcing of herbs, seasonal rituals (ritucharya)

Simple weekly rhythm

  • Mon — Educate (carousel or talking-head Reel)
  • Wed — Show the experience (satisfying treatment Reel)
  • Fri — Proof (testimonial / review) + a soft CTA
  • Daily Stories — polls ("Vata, Pitta or Kapha?"), countdowns, quick tips, re-share client tags

5. Content menu & hooks

Ready-to-shoot ideas — pick from this list each week so there's never a blank page:

  • "POV: your first Shirodhara" (the oil-pour clip)
  • "3 signs your Vata is out of balance"
  • "Can't sleep? Try this Ayurvedic bedtime ritual"
  • "What your tongue says about your health"
  • "Office neck & shoulder pain? This is for KL desk workers"
  • "We asked our Vaidya: what is Panchakarma?"
  • Client testimonial (15-sec, face or voice-over)
  • "A day at Kerala Ayurvedic Lifestyle" (behind-the-scenes)
  • "Which dosha are you? Take our free quiz 👇"
  • Deepavali / Thaipusam wellness gift vouchers
  • "Post-natal care the Ayurvedic way"
  • "How we blend our herbal oils" (sourcing story)

Strong hook = first 2 seconds. Lead with the problem or the satisfying visual, not the logo. Always end with one clear CTA (quiz, WhatsApp, or book).

6. Paid track — ads that book

Keep paid simple: a two-step funnel on Meta (Instagram + Facebook) and TikTok.

LayerObjectiveAudience & creative
1. Awareness / reachCheap views & engagementBoost your best organic Reel to KL, 25–55, interests: wellness, yoga, massage, spa. Goal: video views, page follows.
2. Lead / bookingQuiz leads & bookingsDrive to the free Dosha quiz or a treatment page. Offer creative ("15% off your first treatment"). Optimise for leads / landing-page views.
3. RetargetingClose the warm audienceAnyone who watched a video, visited the site, or started the quiz → show testimonial + offer + WhatsApp. Highest ROI layer.

7. Campaign ideas run one per month

A. "Discover Your Dosha" launch

Hero the free quiz across Reels + Stories + an ad. Quiz result → email capture → personalised treatment offer. Best top-of-funnel campaign because it gives value before asking for a booking.

B. First-visit offer

"15% off your first treatment this month." Public voucher code from admin, promoted on all channels with a deadline for urgency.

C. Refer-a-friend

Activate the referral engine: "You both get RM30 off." Give existing clients a code + QR to share on WhatsApp — turns happy customers into a channel.

D. Corporate wellness (KL offices)

Package Stress & Sleep / neck-&-shoulder therapies for companies in KL Sentral & Bangsar. Outreach via LinkedIn + a one-pager; social case studies as proof.

E. Festival & seasonal

Deepavali / Thaipusam / year-end gift vouchers and "reset" packages. Lean into authentic Kerala heritage — your key differentiator.
